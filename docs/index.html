Rialto learner pool

Hallenbad Rialto has two learner pools; courses use learner pool 2 (LSB2), which is open to the public when it isn't booked. Green: open and free. Click a booking for details.

Open, learner pool 2 free Booked for courses Booked per plan, but maybe not during school holidays Closed

Add to your calendar (subscribe by URL in Google Calendar: Other calendars → + → From URL):
  • rialto.ics: when learner pool 2 is open and free, plus closed days
  • rialto-blocked.ics: only the times learner pool 2 is booked

Bookings come from the official occupancy plan (Belegungsplan) of Hallenbad Rialto. For Knechtli, Delfin and Schwimmschule beider Basel their published lesson dates decide whether a booking takes place on a given day (no lessons during school holidays). Learner pools are assumed to close 30 minutes before the pool. Course sources: Schwimmschule beider Basel, Schwimmschule Knechtli, swimly (Schwimmschule Delfin), bs.ch Hallenbad Rialto.
